# LearnForge compared with LearnHarness — 2 November 2026

**Verdict: LearnForge has credible implementation advantages in question checking and controlled teaching progression. It has not established an overall product or learning-effectiveness lead. LearnHarness remains more developed in persistent knowledge organization, open-ended practice infrastructure, packaged connections and distribution.**

The most useful direction for LearnForge is dependable guided tutoring with visible evidence and useful representations. Adding the same feature names as another product is insufficient.

## Shared or still-unproven areas

- **Question formats:** LearnHarness is not multiple-choice-only; source includes free recall and conversational practice. LearnForge offers choice and rubric-graded written answers plus code practice. Neither comparison establishes general numeric-with-units, algebra-equivalence, ordering/matching or sketch assessment. [#479](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/479) is a product opportunity, not a feature we already fully satisfy.
- **Long-term retention:** LearnForge has optional same-topic FSRS revisits, off by default, and Markdown/Anki export. That is a narrower implementation of part of [#33](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/33)/[#404](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/404), not measured long-term retention or a mature cross-topic practice product. LearnHarness's knowledge graph is not itself an SRS schedule. [Our retention service](../../src/services/learning/retention.ts).
- **Audio:** Our native local dictation and optional ElevenLabs narration address parts of [#477](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/477) and [#189](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/189). Native dictation just required a substantial fix; its lifecycle/UI tests did not use the physical microphone. Count audio as implemented but not a proven reliability advantage.
- **Draft safety:** LearnForge persists question-specific drafts, composer drafts and help/session position. [#459](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/459) describes upstream loss, while 0.4.1 also documents draft improvements. Our localStorage/quota limits and untested crash scenarios prevent a blanket durability win. [Composer](../../src/components/chat/Composer.tsx), [session position](../../src/services/learning/sessionPosition.ts).
- **Formatting and visual quality:** [#467](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/467) reports escaped C code; [#312](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/312) reports mismatched answer letters. LearnForge had its own recent currency/letter/figure defects. Their repairs and screenshots are useful evidence, but are not a matched cross-product formatting benchmark.
- **Theme/accessibility:** Our paper figures have light/dark appearances; the app shell does not have a complete light theme. Do not claim [#444](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/444) is solved app-wide here.
- **Learning effectiveness:** Software tests, scripted learner answers, model judges and issue counts cannot establish real comprehension or retention. No new human baseline/transfer/delayed-learning data was collected.

## Corrections to earlier comparisons

1. [#187](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/187), long-question clipping, is **closed**. Do not present it as an outstanding competitive weakness.
2. [#60](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/60) remains open, but subscription connections are documented as shipped. An open issue is not proof that its original feature is absent.
3. [#58](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/58) requests a **debugger**, not merely execution. Our editor/runners are not a debugger and do not fully satisfy it.
4. [#166](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/166) asks for structured LaTeX/PDF lecture notes. Our Markdown/Anki export is partial coverage, not that feature.
5. Uploaded notes/code in LearnForge are not the current-repository-aware teaching skill requested in [#249](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/249). Topic isolation is also not full independent knowledge profiles as requested in [#207](https://github.com/amosblomqvist/learnharness/issues/207).
6. LearnHarness already teaches with math, visual figures and interactive demos. Our potential advantage is integration, consistency and usable controls, not the existence of those media types.

## What would earn a stronger verdict

| Priority | Concrete work | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Trust the assessment | Evaluate preflight, diagnostic placement, written grading and readiness against independently reviewed questions across technical, numerical and everyday/language goals. Include ambiguous keys, correct answers with wrong reasoning and sensible alternative answers. | Report false approvals/rejections, unresolved outcomes, latency and cost; identify uncovered diagnostic routes. A second model's confidence alone is insufficient. |
| 2 — Complete the loop reliably | Exercise wrong → repair → guided → fresh independent → changed-condition → readiness → later revisit, with timeout, cancellation, reload, draft/help navigation, source-import failure and audio recovery. | Every expected next action is visible; no lost answer, double insertion/grade or credit from supported work. Repeat on the installed Mac build and real providers. |
| 3 — Make representations do teaching work | Choose a diagram/table/model only when it reveals a relevant distinction; connect observations to a prediction or short explanation. Recover gracefully from a failed visual. | The learner changes or inspects a meaningful state, explains the effect and returns with the original task/draft intact. No decorative “interactive” labels. |
| 4 — Compare learning, not feature counts | Run matched, same-model tasks with reviewed answer oracles, then moderated learners with counterbalanced task/app order and a genuine later retrieval visit. | Blindly reviewed explanation/transfer quality, errors, learner assistance, workload and delayed recall. Publish disagreements and failures, not a fabricated overall score. |

The present claim we can defend is: **LearnForge offers a more explicitly controlled adaptive teaching flow with useful assessment safeguards. Whether that produces a better overall experience or better learning than LearnHarness remains unproven.**

## Implementation follow-through: knowledge and practice

The comparison above records the starting snapshot. Subsequent local work adds source-anchored propositions, complete joint-premise reasoning relations, incremental preservation of exact identities, and topic-owned practice runs with original answers, support tracking, tutoring, disputes and resume after reload. See [implementation and verification](../knowledge-practice-release.md). The existing curriculum ledger remains separate; this is not a claim of parity with every LearnHarness graph/practice workflow or evidence of better learning outcomes. Maps refresh explicitly and do not yet continuously infer a learner model from every answer.
